Life status update.

### Hacks 🎤

Recently, I've been watching an American dark comedy-drama called Hacks, and I just couldn't stop watching it. I'm already up to Season 4, and it just keeps getting better and better.

I was first drawn to its unusual award-winning sequence. Apparently, it has won five major awards for every season, and I've seen some clips online that were really good. It's tagged as a dark comedy, but the whole show is actually quite uplifting. I almost feel like picking up my guitar lessons again, which I really should be doing.

Long story short, it's damn good! My favourite characters from the show are Kiki and Jimmy, but honestly, every single character has their own charm. It's an amazing crew.

I actually have two favourite scenes from the show. Wait, make that three.

The first one is when Deborah and Ava are writing jokes together, and Ava is trying out Deborah's fancy facial mask machine that emits lights. Ava asks whether it matters if she keeps her eyes open while using it, and Deborah responds with, "Definitely matters. You are young. You will be fine."

The second one is when Damien finds out that Marcus, his most reliable and overworked colleague, is leaving. He immediately loses all sense of composure and kneels down HAHAHAHAHAHH EVERY LINE HE SAYS IS CLASSIC!

The third one is whenever Kayla yells. I don't know why, but her yelling is just so stress-relieving. It's always at the perfect pitch and perfect volume.

### Work aside 📦
Recently at work, I pitched the idea of spending a day working in the warehouse to learn more about the business processes, specifically how people process orders in the warehouse.

I learned a lot about EDI (Electronic Data Interchange), an old-fashioned but surprisingly efficient and buggy way of sending and receiving wholesale orders. That was a lot of fun, and I really enjoyed it, including the part where I drew a tree diagram. It feels good to know what you're doing!

Cheers to living a beautiful life!
